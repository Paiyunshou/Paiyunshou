## 🆕 Now building: a non-trade payment & withholding system on multidimensional tables

Three linked tables — non-resident supplier registry, contract filing, and withholding detail — strung into one workflow: business submits, finance creates the detail row, tax handles filing and withholds, treasury pays. Ten automated notifications and status write-backs replace manual chasing. The core is the tax judgement and the reinstatement of the tax base: resident-borne ÷ (1 + VAT rate), China-entity-borne ÷ (1 − income tax rate), then multiplied by the rate.
